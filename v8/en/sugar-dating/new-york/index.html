Sugar dating in New York

Nobody looks twice here, and a first drink is never far from anyone's office.

Join

New Yorkers work long hours and guard their free time, which is why clear expectations appeal: less guessing, more evenings worth having. In a city this size, nobody notices who you're having dinner with. Still, choose a place away from your office and your usual spots.

Good spots for a first date

For the first meeting, choose a calm public place where you can hear each other. These neighborhoods work well:

  • The West Village: small restaurants and wine bars on quiet streets
  • The Upper East Side: cafés near the museums, calm and classic
  • Midtown hotel lounges: easy to find, discreet, good for a first drink
  • Brooklyn Heights and DUMBO: a walk with a view of Manhattan

Practical tips

The subway is usually faster than a cab, but late in the evening a car is more comfortable. Book ahead from Thursday to Saturday; popular places fill up quickly.

Meeting safely

  • Meet in a public place the first time, during the day or early evening.
  • Arrange your own transportation, there and back.
  • Tell someone you trust where you'll be and who you're meeting.
  • Share your address, employer or last name only when you're ready.
  • Discuss expectations in chat beforehand, not for the first time at the table.
