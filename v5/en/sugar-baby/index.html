What is a sugar baby?

A sugar baby is someone who wants a relationship with an older, established partner and is open about it. Sugar babies are students, young professionals, creatives and entrepreneurs. What they share: they know what they want and set their own boundaries.

What you get depends on what you agree on: attention, company, a mentor, new places or support with your plans. Read more in What is sugar dating.

Building a strong profile

  • Choose a username that doesn't point to your real name or socials.
  • Describe who you are and what you're looking for in a few sentences.
  • Be clear about your boundaries. It saves trouble later.
  • Use recent photos that look like you, but that you don't use on other profiles.

You set the pace

You're never obligated to anything. Take time to get to know someone in chat first, and only meet when it feels right. Anyone who pressures you or asks for money or personal details up front isn't worth your time.

Meeting safely

  • Meet in a public place for the first date, during the day or early evening.
  • Arrange your own transportation there and back.
  • Tell someone you trust where you'll be and who you're meeting.
  • Share your address, employer or last name only when you're ready.
  • Talk about expectations in chat beforehand, not for the first time at the table.
