What is sugar dating?

An honest explanation: what it is, how it works and what to watch for.

I'm a sugar babyI'm a sugar daddy / mommy

The short version

Sugar dating is dating where two adults openly discuss what they expect from each other up front. Usually it's an established, older partner (the sugar daddy or sugar mommy) and a younger partner (the sugar baby). One brings time, experience and generosity; the other brings company, energy and a fresh perspective.

How it works

You create a profile, say what you're looking for and get to know someone in chat. If it clicks, you meet. The difference with regular dating is that expectations don't stay unspoken. See step by step in How it works.

What is an arrangement?

An arrangement is what you agree on together: how often you see each other, what you do and what you mean to each other. It can be anything from the occasional dinner to traveling together or a steady mentor.

  • Bring it up early, in chat
  • Be specific: frequency, activities, expectations
  • Agree on how and when you'll check in
  • Anyone can stop at any time

Safety

Meet in public the first time, arrange your own ride and share personal details only when you're ready. More tips on our safety page.

How it differs from escorting

With escorting, you pay for a service, by the hour or per appointment. Sugar dating is a relationship between two people who get to know each other and make agreements together. SugarDates is a dating site: offering or asking for sex for money isn't allowed, and profiles that do are removed.
