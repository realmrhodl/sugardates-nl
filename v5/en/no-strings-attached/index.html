What does it mean?

No strings attached, or NSA, means spending time together without obligations for the future. No expectations of a relationship, but clarity about what you both want now.

Keeping it clear

  • Say up front that you're not looking for commitment
  • Agree on how often you'll meet and stay in touch
  • Respect each other's schedule and boundaries
  • Be honest if what you want changes
