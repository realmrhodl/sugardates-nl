Sugar daddy / mommy

What is a sugar daddy or sugar mommy?

Someone successful who enjoys spending time with fun, energetic company. You're open about what you're looking for and what you can offer.

  • Company for dinners, events and travel
  • Supporting or mentoring someone
  • Clear agreements up front

Sugar baby

What is a sugar baby?

Someone independent who knows what they want. For example a student, young professional or entrepreneur. You set your boundaries.

  • An experienced mentor to talk to
  • New places and experiences
  • Support that fits what you agree on

Questions

Frequently asked questions

What is sugar dating?
Dating where you're upfront about what you expect from each other, such as companionship, mentoring or support. What you agree on is up to the two of you.
Is SugarDates live yet?
Not yet. You can join the waitlist now, and we'll email you at launch.
How old do I have to be?
You must be 18 or older. We ask for your birth year and you confirm you're 18+.
Do I have to use my real name?
No. You choose a username. Your email isn't shown to other members.
What does it cost?
Joining is free. Some features use credits. We'll publish prices before launch.
What happens to my data?
We don't make your data public. You can ask us to delete your signup at any time. See our privacy policy.
Is this an escort site?
No. SugarDates is a dating site. Offering or asking for sex for money isn't allowed.
