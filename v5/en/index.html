FAQ

Good to know

What is sugar dating?
A way of dating where both partners openly discuss what they expect from each other up front, such as companionship, mentorship or support. What you agree on is up to the two of you.
Is SugarDates live yet?
Not yet. You can join the waitlist now, and we'll email you at launch.
Why 21+?
We've chosen a minimum age of 21. When you sign up, we ask for your birth year and a confirmation.
Do I have to use my real name?
No. You choose a display name, and a pseudonym is fine. Your email address isn't shown to other members.
What does it cost?
Joining is free. Paid features will use credits, and we'll publish the rates before launch.
What happens to my data?
Your data is yours and isn't made public. You can ask us to delete your signup at any time. See our privacy policy for details.
Is SugarDates an escort service?
No. SugarDates is a dating platform. Offering or requesting sex for money isn't allowed.
