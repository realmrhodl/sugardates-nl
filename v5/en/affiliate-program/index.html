Payouts

For the first twelve months after a member's first purchase, you earn 75% on every paid purchase through your link. After that, 45% on later purchases by that member. Here's how it works:

  • Commission is calculated on the purchase amount excluding sales tax or VAT.
  • Commission is never higher than what SugarDates keeps on that purchase after payment-provider fees and any chargebacks.
  • A click stays attributed to you for 60 days. If someone buys within that window, commission also applies to later purchases by that member.
  • Payout within 60 days, worldwide, in euros or US dollars — matching the domain of the purchase.
  • Minimum payout of €50, or the equivalent in dollars. No maximum.
  • If a purchase is refunded or charged back, we deduct that commission from a later payout.

Terms

  • Worldwide, adult traffic only.
  • No misleading claims, no spam, and no paid or incentivized sign-ups.
  • No commission on your own purchases, or those of people in your own organization.
  • No ads on the SugarDates brand name without our written permission.
  • We review every application before approval, and we can end a partnership in case of abuse.

Yes. 75% in the first year, then 45%, for as long as that member is attributed to your link.

We deduct that commission from a later payout.
Can I join from my country?
Yes. The program is available worldwide.
Is there a fixed amount per sign-up?
No. You only earn commission on actual paid purchases — not on a sign-up alone.
