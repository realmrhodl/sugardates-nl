How it works

From signing up to a first date, in four steps.

Join

In four steps

  1. 1

    Sign up

    Choose your role (sugar baby or sugar daddy), a username, your state and your birth year. Then your email address. A nickname is enough.

  2. 2

    Finish your profile

    Say who you're looking for and what for: an ongoing arrangement, meeting now and then, mentoring or travel. Write a short intro. The more specific, the better the match.

  3. 3

    Browse profiles near you

    You'll see profiles of people looking for what you offer, and vice versa. Your location shows as a city or state, never an address.

  4. 4

    Get to know each other

    Send messages and discuss expectations. Meet only when it feels right for both of you. Messages use credits; see Cost.

Good to know

  • Other members can't see your email
  • SugarDates works in your browser; there's no app to install
  • You can delete your account at any time
