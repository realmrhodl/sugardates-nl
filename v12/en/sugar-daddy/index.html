What is a sugar daddy?

Usually a man who has done well in his career, has little time for endless dating and knows exactly what he wants: company, good conversation, someone to share great experiences with. He's generous, with his time, his network or in a way you agree on together.

The difference from other dating sites is openness. You say up front what you're looking for and what you can offer, so nobody has to guess. More in What is sugar dating.

What sugar daddies look for

  • Company for dinners, events and travel
  • Someone to mentor, or to share a network with
  • A connection without unspoken expectations
  • Discretion around your name and your work

How to find a sugar baby who fits

  1. Create a profile with a username and your state. Your real name isn't needed.
  2. Write briefly and honestly what you're looking for. Specific beats vague.
  3. Read profiles carefully and reply personally, not with a template.
  4. Discuss expectations in chat before you meet.
  5. Meet in a public place the first time.

Good arrangements

An arrangement works when you both know where you stand: how often you meet, what you do together and what you expect from each other. Keep it respectful and leave room to adjust. SugarDates is a dating site; sex for money doesn't belong here.
