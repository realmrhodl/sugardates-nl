What is an arrangement?

The agreement two people make about their relationship: how often you meet, what you do together and what you mean to each other. It can be about time and attention, outings and travel, and sometimes support.

Forms of support

Some people agree that one partner supports the other, with a monthly allowance, per date, or through gifts and experiences. What you agree on is up to the two of you. Support is part of a relationship between two people, not a service. SugarDates is a dating site. Offering or asking for sex for money isn't allowed, and profiles that do are removed.

A good arrangement

  • Is specific: how often, what, and what you expect
  • Is discussed up front, not afterward
  • Leaves room to adjust
  • Can be ended by either of you at any time
