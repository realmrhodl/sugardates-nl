Dating while married

A question we hear often: how discreet is it? A factual answer.

Can I use SugarDates?

SugarDates is for adults who know what they want. Your situation at home, and how you handle it, is up to you. We do ask for honesty toward the people you meet here: share your situation in your profile or in chat so they can make their own choice.

How discreet is it?

  • You choose a username; your real name isn't needed
  • You decide which photos to show
  • Your location shows as a city or state, never an address
  • Other members can't see your email
  • No app needed: SugarDates works in your browser
  • You can delete your account at any time

What we don't promise

No site can guarantee nobody will recognize you. Someone you know could come across your profile, so keep that in mind when choosing what to share. See Discreet dating for tips.

Can I date if I'm married?

SugarDates is a dating site for adults. We don't ask about your marital status, but we do ask for honesty toward the people you meet here.

Is SugarDates discreet?

You use a username, choose which photos to show, your location only shows as a city or state and other members can't see your email.
