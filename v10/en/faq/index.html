Frequently asked questions

The questions we hear most, answered briefly.

Join
What is sugar dating?

Dating where you're upfront about what you expect from each other, such as companionship, mentoring or support. What you agree on is up to the two of you.

What's the difference between a sugar daddy and a sugar baby?

A sugar daddy is the established partner, who offers time, experience and generosity. A sugar baby is the younger partner: independent, with plans of her own, looking for someone experienced.

What does SugarDates cost?

Creating an account and filling in your profile is free. Some features, like sending messages, use credits. No subscription. See Cost.

Do I have to use my real name?

No. You choose a username. Your email isn't shown to other members.

Is my location shown?

Only your city or state, never your address.

Do I need an app?

No. SugarDates works in the browser on your phone, tablet and computer.

How do I meet safely?

Meet in a public place the first time, arrange your own ride and tell someone where you are. More on our safety page.

Is SugarDates an escort site?

No. SugarDates is a dating site. Offering or asking for sex for money isn't allowed.

Can I delete my account?

Yes, you can delete your account at any time.

Which countries does SugarDates cover?

SugarDates.net is for the United States. SugarDates.nl is the Dutch-language site for the Netherlands and Belgium. Both run on the same platform.
