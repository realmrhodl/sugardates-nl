Safety and privacy

Discretion starts with you. Here's how to stay safe, online and off.

Join

Your profile

  • Pick a username that doesn't lead to your real name or socials
  • Use photos you don't also have on Instagram or LinkedIn
  • Don't mention your employer, street or other identifying details
  • Your location only shows as a city or state

In chat

  • Keep the conversation on SugarDates until you trust someone
  • Don't share your phone number, address or last name in early messages
  • Take your time; people in a hurry usually have a reason

Spotting scams

Be careful if someone asks for money, gift cards, crypto or bank details up front, wants to move you to another app quickly, or has a story that sounds too good to be true. End the conversation.

The first date

  • Meet in a public place the first time, during the day or early evening.
  • Arrange your own transportation, there and back.
  • Tell someone you trust where you'll be and who you're meeting.
  • Share your address, employer or last name only when you're ready.
  • Discuss expectations in chat beforehand, not for the first time at the table.

How SugarDates handles your data

We don't make your data public, and other members can't see your email. You can delete your account at any time. SugarDates is a dating site; sex for money isn't allowed. See also the FAQ.
